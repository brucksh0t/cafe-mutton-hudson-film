## Motion (illustrative, generated, not filmed)
Each film is image-to-video generated with the restaurant's photo as the first frame and a crop / gentle rotation of the same photo (or, for the glide, the second photo) as the last frame, then 2x upscaled with a desktop video upscaler. Every film carries the on-screen label "Illustrative motion generated from Cafe Mutton's photos, not filmed".

| Film | Frames | First frame → last frame |
|---|---|---|
| hero (walk up to the blue door) | 81 | storefront photo, 16:9 crop → tighter crop on the door and sign |
| pie ("CLOSE" aperture, slow descent) | 65 | pie photo rotated 90° (overhead) → closer, turned 8° |
| glide (camera crosses the table) | 81 | fish photo → terrine photo. The strip of table between the two plates is generated: the two plates were photographed separately. |
| brunch (descent to the egg) | 65 | porridge photo → closer, turned 6° |

Rejected after frame-by-frame review: a skate push-in (a bright glare blob slid across the fish), a terrine push-in (generated sun patches on the table and plate), and a figurine push-in (the ceramic dog's painted eyes changed shape; soft source).

Frames: per-frame AVIF at the lowest quality whose luma SSIM against the lossless master meets a target (0.97 phone, 0.965 desktop, 0.96 large), quality floor 26, with WebP fallback; tiers m 1080×1440 (true crops of the 2x frames, no resize), d 1920×1080, x 2560×1440, picked from canvas CSS size × DPR (cap 2). A light WebP preview ladder (every 3rd frame) lands first. Nothing in frames/ is requested before the first scroll. Save-Data or 2G: stills instead of films. Reduced motion or no JS: stills and all content, nothing scaled above 1x.
